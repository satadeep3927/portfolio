Intelligent knowledge management system with advanced cognitive capabilities for information retrieval and organization. CogniBase combines AI/ML techniques with practical knowledge management to enable semantic search and intelligent document processing.

Designed for organizations requiring sophisticated knowledge extraction and retrieval from large document repositories.

### Key Capabilities

- **Semantic Search**: AI-powered search with contextual understanding and relevance scoring
- **Knowledge Extraction**: Automatic extraction and structuring of information from unstructured documents
- **Natural Language Processing**: Advanced NLP for query understanding and document analysis
- **Cognitive Analytics**: Pattern recognition and insight generation from knowledge graphs
- **Scalable Architecture**: Distributed design supporting large-scale enterprise knowledge repositories
